Nutrition & Diet

Simple, sustainable eating to match your training.

What Does a Nutritionist Do?
  • Guides healthy eating, creates personalized meal plans, educates on nutrients.
  • Credentials: Registered Dietitian (RD), evidence-based advice.
  • Sample advice: Balanced diet with protein, carbs, fats, vitamins, minerals, and fiber.
  • Benefits: Disease prevention, improved energy, better digestion, sustainable habits.
  • Example: British Nutrition Foundation offers training and resources for healthy eating.
