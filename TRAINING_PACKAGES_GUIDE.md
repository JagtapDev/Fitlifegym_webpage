## 💪 MUSCLE BUILDING STRATEGY

### Training Protocol:
- **4-5 Day Split Workout Routine**
  - Day 1: Chest
  - Day 2: Back
  - Day 3: Shoulders
  - Day 4: Legs
  - Day 5: Arms/Core

- **Focus on Compound Exercises:**
  - Deadlifts
  - Squats
  - Bench Press
  - Barbell Rows
  - Overhead Press

- **Rep Range:** 8-12 reps per set for hypertrophy
- **Progressive Overload:** Increase weight every 2 weeks
- **Rest Period:** 48-72 hours between same muscle groups

### Nutrition for Muscle Growth:

| Nutrient | Daily Requirement |
|----------|------------------|
| Protein | 1.6-2.2g per kg of body weight |
| Calories | 300-500 above maintenance |
| Carbs | 4-6g per kg |
| Healthy Fats | 0.5-1.5g per kg |
| Water | 3-4 liters daily |

### Sample Muscle Building Diet (Daily):

**Breakfast:**
- 4 Eggs
- Oatmeal (100g)
- Banana
- Almonds

**Mid-morning:**
- Protein Shake (30g)
- Apple

**Lunch:**
- Chicken Breast (200g)
- Rice (150g)
- Broccoli

**Pre-workout:**
- Banana
- Peanut Butter

**Post-workout:**
- Protein Shake
- Glucose/Dextrose

**Dinner:**
- Fish or Mutton (250g)
- Sweet Potato
- Mixed Salad

**Before Bed:**
- Cottage Cheese
- Berries

### Recovery & Supplementation:
- **Sleep:** 7-9 hours minimum
- **Supplements:**
  - Whey Protein
  - Creatine Monohydrate
  - Multivitamins
  - Omega-3 Fish Oil
- **Stretching & Foam Rolling:** 10 mins daily
- **Active Recovery Days:** Light cardio or yoga
- **Progress Tracking:** Weekly measurements

## ⚖️ WEIGHT LOSS APPROACH

### Cardio & Training Protocol:

- **Cardio Sessions:** 4-5 days/week (30-45 mins)
  - Running
  - Cycling
  - Swimming
  - Rowing

- **Strength Training:** 2-3 days/week (lighter weights, high reps)
- **HIIT Workouts:** 20-30 mins, 2x per week
- **Daily Steps:** 10,000+ steps
- **Rest Days:** Essential for recovery

### Nutrition for Fat Loss:

| Nutrient | Daily Requirement |
|----------|------------------|
| Calories | 300-500 below maintenance |
| Protein | 1.8-2.2g per kg (preserve muscle) |
| Carbs | 2-3g per kg (mainly vegetables) |
| Fats | 0.5-1g per kg (healthy sources) |
| Fiber | 25-35g daily |
| Water | 3-4 liters daily |

### Sample Weight Loss Diet (Daily):

**Breakfast:**
- 3 Eggs
- Whole Wheat Toast (2 slices)
- Green Tea

**Mid-morning:**
- Green Apple
- Almonds (10 pieces)

**Lunch:**
- Grilled Chicken (150g)
- Brown Rice (80g)
- Mixed Vegetables

**Afternoon Snack:**
- Greek Yogurt (100g)
- Honey (1 tbsp)

**Pre-workout:**
- Small Banana

**Dinner:**
- Fish or Tofu (180g)
- Large Salad
- Lentil Soup

**Hydration:**
- 3-4 liters water
- 2-3 cups green tea

### Lifestyle Changes for Weight Loss:
- **Sleep:** 7-8 hours (affects hunger hormones)
- **Stress Management:** Meditation, yoga, breathing exercises
- **Meal Prep:** Plan meals for the entire week
- **Calorie Tracking:** Use fitness apps for accountability
- **Weekly Weigh-ins:** Track progress
- **Avoid:** Sugary drinks, processed foods, late-night eating
- **Consistency:** Stick to routine for 12+ weeks minimum

## 🎯 BALANCED FITNESS APPROACH

### Training Split:
- **3 Days:** Strength Training (compound movements)
- **2 Days:** Cardio/HIIT Sessions
- **2 Days:** Rest or Active Recovery (yoga, stretching)

### Nutrition:
- **Protein:** 1.6-1.8g per kg
- **Calories:** Maintenance or slight deficit
- **Carbs:** 3-4g per kg (timed around workouts)
- **Fats:** 0.7-1g per kg
- **Philosophy:** 80/20 rule - 80% clean, 20% flexibility

### Wellness Components:
- **Meditation:** 10-15 mins daily
- **Sleep:** Consistent 8-hour schedule
- **Flexibility Work:** Yoga 2x per week
- **Social Fitness:** Group classes for motivation
- **Mindful Eating:** No skipping meals

## 💡 HOW TO CUSTOMIZE

### Edit Pricing:
Edit the `packages` array in `TrainingPackages.jsx`:
```javascript
{
  id: 1,
  name: 'Starter Package',
  duration: '1 Month',
  priceINR: '₹4,999',  // Change this
  priceUSD: '$60',     // And this
  // ... rest of package details
}
```

### Add New Strategy:
Add to the `strategies` array with your content:
```javascript
{
  id: 'your-strategy',
  title: '📌 YOUR STRATEGY',
  icon: '🎯',
  content: [ /* your content */ ]
}
```

### Update Services:
Modify the `additionalServices` array for add-on offerings.
