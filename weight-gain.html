Science-Backed Weight Gain Tips

How to Gain Weight Effectively
  • Calorie surplus: eat more than you burn, focus on nutrient-dense foods.
  • Sample foods: meats, fish, eggs, dairy, legumes, nuts, oats, quinoa, sweet potatoes, dried fruit, olive oil, avocados.
  • Exercise: Resistance training (weights, bodyweight), consider protein supplements.
  • Tips: Use bigger plates, avoid water before meals, get quality sleep, consider weight gainer shakes if needed.
  • Benefits: Build muscle, improve strength, support healthy metabolism, avoid unhealthy fat gain.
