Science-Backed Weight Loss Tips

How to Lose Weight Effectively
  • Eat balanced meals: protein, healthy fats, vegetables, and complex carbs.
  • Sample meal: poached egg, avocado, berries; salmon with ginger and zucchini; spinach, mushroom, and feta crustless quiche.
  • Exercise: Combine cardio (walking, cycling, swimming) with strength training (weights, resistance bands, bodyweight).
  • Eat mindfully, stay hydrated, get enough sleep, set realistic goals, track progress.
  • Benefits: Lower risk of heart disease, diabetes, improved mood, better sleep, sustainable results.
